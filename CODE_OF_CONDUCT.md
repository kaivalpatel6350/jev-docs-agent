# Code of Conduct

We want Jev Docs Agent to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Keep feedback about the work, constructive and specific.
- Credit others' ideas and contributions.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or imagery.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sharing credentials, private recordings or other people's data.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and PRs, and may block contributors who break these rules.
Report problems privately to the maintainer via [GitHub](https://github.com/kaivalpatel6350). Reports are handled
confidentially.

Inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
